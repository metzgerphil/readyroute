# Ready Route Answers v2 workbench

This is the clean authoring workspace for the replacement corpus. Version 1 remains an archive rather than an active authority.

Place original source material in `sources/`, which remains excluded from Git because it may contain restricted material. Register every accepted source in `inventory/source_inventory.csv`. Author evidence-backed records in `knowledge/records.jsonl`, then add evaluation cases under `validation/`.

Do not bulk-restore records, evidence summaries, aliases, or answer text from the v1 archive. Historical material may be selectively recovered when the product owner authorizes it. Documentary claims should be checked against original bytes. Information explicitly supplied or confirmed by the product owner may instead follow the owner-verification path and become `READY_ROUTE_APPROVED` without a matching external document.

Owner verification is not permission to guess. Preserve the exact supplied procedure, stated conditions, approver, date, and reopen conditions. Anything not supplied remains a gap.

The first selective recovery occurred on 2026-08-15 using the partner handoff dated 2026-08-14. The original PDFs were re-preserved in the private source area and the relevant pages were re-reviewed before any record became eligible.
