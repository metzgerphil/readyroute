# Ready Route human-verification questionnaire — deferred controlling sources

Status date: 2026-08-10

## Instructions for the respondent

Answer for the current United States FedEx Ground P&D operation. For each section, provide:

- respondent role/team and the operational area they are authorized to verify;
- answer date and effective date of the procedure;
- whether the answer is national or varies by station, state, service provider, vehicle class, or customer;
- the exact current procedure, including ordered actions, required documentation, prohibitions, and escalation;
- `UNKNOWN` or `REQUIRES LOCAL MANAGEMENT` where no national answer exists—do not guess;
- source/form identifier and revision if known.

A respondent may answer only the sections within their authority. Ready Route will preserve every limit and will not generalize a local practice into a national procedure.

## A. Delivery status codes — OP-324

Recommended respondent: current FedEx P&D operations owner or station operations manager authorized to verify national status-code use.

1. What is the current U.S. Ground revision/effective date of OP-324?
2. Provide the complete current delivery-status-code list with each exact code number and label.
3. For every driver-selectable code, what exact condition permits its use, and what similar condition must not use it?
4. For every driver-selectable code, what must the driver do before and after applying it: scan, attempt, contact, comment, door tag, package marking, custody, return location, or management notification?
5. Which codes are automatically applied by a system and must never be manually selected by a driver?
6. Which codes require management/CXPC approval or a second attempt, and what evidence or documentation is required?
7. Specifically confirm the complete current procedure for ordinary recipient refusal/code 006, including package disposition and documentation after the code is applied.

## B. Pickup reason codes — OP-321

## C. Manual delivery records — OP-207 and OP-207Res

Recommended respondent: current FedEx P&D operations owner familiar with scanner/FORGE outages and HAL delivery.

18. What does the driver do with the package and completed form after the stop: custody, copies, station return, check-in, submission, retention, and system reconciliation?
19. Is “Blue Sheet” an approved name for either current form, an informal local term, or a different document?
20. What must the driver do if the form is unavailable, damaged, incomplete, or the scanner resumes working before check-in?
