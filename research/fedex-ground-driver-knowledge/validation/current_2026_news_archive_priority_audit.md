# Current 2026 news-archive priority audit

Audit date: 2026-08-09

## Purpose

This targeted pass tests whether the eight captured 2026 monthly archive result pages expose clear, current, broadly applicable P&D driver procedures that should outrank the remaining Tier 1 controlling-document work. It does not treat an archive result title, date, summary, or link as evidence of an article's operational contents.

Authenticated DOM captures exist for `MGB-ARCHIVE-0001` through `MGB-ARCHIVE-0008`, labeled August 2026 through January 2026. Together the result pages expose 15 result entries:

- January: truck-driving competitions plus a November 2025 QVABV/Equipment Terms notice.

## Findings

1. The archive filters do not produce a clean month-only current corpus. Several 2026-labeled archive pages contain 2024 or 2025 results, so archive label and query parameters cannot prove article date, currency, or supersession.
2. The current customer-alert rollup is already fully reviewed in its separate time-aware alert layer.
3. CSCA, surveys, bidding, fuel discounts, competitions, Network 2.0, FRO reporting, and facility forums are administrative, system-management, promotional, or contractor-planning material at the result-title level. Their titles do not justify driver-procedure extraction.
4. The Mother's Day perishables result is seasonal and event-dated; it cannot establish an enduring current procedure without article review and effective-period analysis.
5. The QVABV/Equipment Terms result points back toward the already prioritized current Equipment Terms family; the result title cannot complete that document or establish its terms.
6. The historical drop-box/FCC video set has completed audio-visual review and is retained only as 2017 manager-facing context; it does not create current canonical mappings or displace the current OP-321 and pickup-document targets.

## Priority decision

This archive sample produced no new source-complete, broadly applicable current driver procedure. The captures are preserved as discovery artifacts, but review remains deferred behind OP-135 completion, current pickup materials, OP-324/OP-321, OP-207/OP-207Res, HZ-035, SF-920P, the current ERG, Driver Safety Guidebook, Company Safety and Operation Handbook, and current Equipment Terms.

This is a diminishing-yield finding for the focused current-news lane, not proof that every linked article is irrelevant. Article content remains unreviewed and cannot support canonical knowledge until separately acquired and assessed.
