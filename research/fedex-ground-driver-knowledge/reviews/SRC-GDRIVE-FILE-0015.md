# Source review: SRC-GDRIVE-FILE-0015

## Review result

- Status: `FULLY_REVIEWED`
- Method: page-marked text extraction plus visual review of both rendered pages
- Source date: 2/13/25
- Scope: customer feedback and quick-reference package-placement considerations
- Limitation: much of the document is framed as customer expectations or considerations, not unconditional operational commands. The knowledge layer must preserve that distinction.

## Page coverage and findings

- Page 1 contains photographs of customer-reported placement that did not meet expectations; it provides examples, not a complete rule set.
- Page 2 covers apartments, garage doors, long driveways, doorways/ramps, weather/theft protection, large packages, driver release, and PPOD quality.
- Apartment guidance distinguishes delivery to the apartment when the office cannot receive and the customer is home from leaving a door tag when the customer is not home.
- Customer feedback disfavors garage-door placement, expects primary-entrance delivery even on long driveways, and expects accessibility and door operation not to be obstructed.
- Driver-release checks include point-of-delivery scanning, any applicable name/signature, accurate status codes, and consideration of a door tag when release is not possible.
- PPOD checks prohibit people, addresses, label data, and other identifying information in the image; the photo should show the placed package, a secure location, and useful surroundings.
