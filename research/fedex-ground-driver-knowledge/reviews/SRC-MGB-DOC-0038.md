## Extracted operational scope

Two distinct current safety branches are modeled:

1. Stop on red; do not run a red light.
2. Prevent sideswipes through safe speed, ideal-condition following-distance timing, frequent mirror/blind-spot monitoring, double-checks when turning, advance signaling, and adjacent-traffic/hazard scanning before a lane change.
