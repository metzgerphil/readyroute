## Review result

Federal Register page 55889 contains the operative FMCSA decision renewing the TRALA exemption from 49 CFR 395.8(a)(1)(i). The exemption is effective from October 12, 2022 through October 12, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. local time unless renewed or rescinded. It covers property-carrying CMVs rented for eight days or fewer and excuses installation and use of an ELD for RODS during that qualifying rental period.

The same page establishes these material conditions:

- all other applicable Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations still apply;
- replacing rental CMVs or renewing the same agreement in eight-day cycles to avoid ELD use violates the exemption;
- the driver must possess a copy of the exemption notice;
- the driver must carry the rental agreement identifying the parties, vehicle, and rental dates;
- the driver must possess current-day and prior-seven-day RODS when required;
- conflicting state rules are preempted for interstate operation during the exemption, but states are not required to adopt the exemption for intrastate operation; and
- the carrier has a separate five-business-day FMCSA accident-notification obligation for accidents involving operation under the exemption.

The second PDF page is the continuation of unrelated Federal Railroad Administration notices. It supplies no Ready Route operational evidence.

## Interpretation limits

This source establishes the federal exemption, not the complete FedEx Ground implementation. It does not establish the approved Motive/manual-entry workflow, the internal pre-dispatch approval owner, or whether a particular intrastate operation is covered. The 2027 expiration must remain a currency gate. `KNO-HOS-RENTAL-ELD-001` therefore remains human-review required.
