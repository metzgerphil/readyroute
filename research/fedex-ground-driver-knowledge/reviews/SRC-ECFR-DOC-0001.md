## Review result

Section 396.9(d)(1) requires a driver who receives an inspection report to deliver a copy to the motor carrier operating the vehicle and, when applicable, the intermodal equipment provider upon arrival at the next terminal or facility. If the driver is not scheduled to reach the relevant terminal or facility within 24 hours, the driver must immediately transmit the report.

Related provisions establish that:

- a vehicle or intermodal equipment declared and marked out of service may not be operated until the required repairs are satisfactorily completed;
- the carrier or intermodal equipment provider must examine the report and correct listed violations or defects;
- within 15 days, the responsible entity must certify correction, return the form to the issuing State agency if requested, and retain the required copy; and
- §396.9(d)(3)(ii) specifies 12-month retention for the roadside inspection report copy.

## Interpretation limits

The eCFR is an authoritative but unofficial editorial compilation of federal regulations. This snapshot establishes the federal baseline, but it does not identify FedEx's internal recipient, service-provider reporting chain, citation/qualification workflow, or additional state obligations. It also must not be confused with the separate driver vehicle inspection report retention rule elsewhere in Part 396. `KNO-DOT-ROADSIDE-REPORT-001` therefore remains human-review required.
