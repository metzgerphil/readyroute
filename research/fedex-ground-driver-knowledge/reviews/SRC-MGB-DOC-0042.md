# SRC-MGB-DOC-0042 — FORGE Download Pickup List

- Review date: 2026-08-10
- Version: 2.2.0, revised 6/10/2025
- Coverage: complete one-page original PDF; text extracted and full page visually reviewed
- Status: fully reviewed

The guide establishes that an on-road pickup-list request is based on the logged-in work area and requires cellular connectivity. `Download Pickup List` appears in the Stop List menu only when no pickup list was received during login. Selecting it requests the list; `OK` returns to the Stop List with downloaded pickups. Ground empty-list results say to try again later, while Express no-list results say no valid pickup listing was found. Unlisted pickups completed before the download may be reconciled at End of Day. The guide does not define broader outage authority or guarantee that a list exists.
