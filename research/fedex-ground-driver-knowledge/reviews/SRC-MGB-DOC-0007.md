# SRC-MGB-DOC-0007 — OP-132 Passenger in Other Vehicle or Witness

- Review date: 2026-08-08
- Document revision: 8/2025
- Coverage: 1/1 page, visual review
- Status: fully reviewed

The form is to be completed by a passenger in another vehicle or a witness, with driver assistance. It captures date, time, accident location, city/province/state, whether the person was the accident driver, injury status, passengers/witnesses, name, address, telephone, remarks, signature, and signing contact/address information.
