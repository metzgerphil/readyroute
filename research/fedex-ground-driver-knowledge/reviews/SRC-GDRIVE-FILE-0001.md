## Evidence-sensitive findings

- Page 1 distinguishes three signature services. ISR can be satisfied at the delivery address, at a neighboring address with a door tag left at the delivery address, or through a signature on a listed OP-200 form. DSR requires an in-person signature at the delivery address. ASR requires ID-based age confirmation and an in-person signature from a person 21 or older.
- Page 1 limits unattended residential release to packages with no signature requirement, at a qualifying residence, in a secure/protected location near the primary entrance, outside a USPS mailbox, and consistent with reasonable delivery instructions.
- Page 1 says commercial and signature-service deliveries are expected not to be left unattended; commercial signature may be waived by OP-201.
- Page 1 describes scanning at the customer location and at the time of each pickup, delivery, or attempt. It identifies pre-scanning, late scanning, incorrect residential/commercial designation, delivery-as-pickup designation, and inapplicable codes as inaccurate information.
- Page 1 identifies same-day second-attempt expectations for exact source labels FO, PA, PA+, and M&I. The source does not define those abbreviations, so the branch is applied only to an explicitly matching displayed label and the abbreviations are not expanded or generalized.
- Page 2 separately establishes current on-route security expectations above the status tables: commercially reasonable measures for people/package/vehicle security, locked doors (including bulkhead where applicable), closed windows between stops, and removed or lock-box-secured keys when the vehicle is not operating.
- Page 2 supplies code labels only. The labels are indexed for discovery but are not independently sufficient to answer when a particular code must be used.
