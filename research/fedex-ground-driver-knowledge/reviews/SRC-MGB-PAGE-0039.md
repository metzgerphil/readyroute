# SRC-MGB-PAGE-0039 — Standards for P&D Road Tests

- Review date: 2026-08-09

- Status: fully reviewed
- Durable capture: `captures/mygroundbiz/pd_road_tests.txt`

The page requires a DOT road test by a Qualified Provider or designated Certified Trainer before a P&D driver candidate is assigned to provide service. It establishes administrator conditions, prerequisite completion of the initial documentation steps and defensive-driver training, and three test components: pre-trip inspection, driving range/obstacle course, and at least ten miles of on-road driving. The applicable Qualified Provider-supplied Record of Road Test documents the observation.

The linked Qualification Conditions document and complete Qualified Provider form/route instructions remain separately unreviewed. The page supports the minimum gate and test structure but not unseen form fields or all qualification-law interpretations.
