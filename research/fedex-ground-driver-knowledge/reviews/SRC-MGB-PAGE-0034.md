# SRC-MGB-PAGE-0034 — Alternative Vehicle Operations

- Review date: 2026-08-09

- Status: fully reviewed

The page defines Alternative Vehicles as personal or passenger-type vehicles whose operators need only meet basic qualification standards. It establishes a minimum onboarding sequence: service-provider interest through station management; completed Statement of Lease with service-provider and authorized-station signatures; Statement of Sub-Lease when the service provider is not the owner; other registration, inspection, insurance, and photo materials; then assignment of a Unit number and provision of a dashboard placard. The page states that the vehicle is ready to provide services after the Unit number and placard are provided.

The page does not expose the contents of the two forms, the four named MBA technical guides, the insurance FAQ, or underlying Agreement terms. Those remain separate acquisition obligations and are not inferred.
