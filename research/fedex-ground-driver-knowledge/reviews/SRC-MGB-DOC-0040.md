# SRC-MGB-DOC-0040 — Vehicle Appearance and Brand Promotion FAQ

- Review date: 2026-08-10
- Portal update label: 2026-01-02
- Coverage: 2/2 pages from archived original PDF
- Status: fully reviewed

Page 1 explains changes in decal size, placement, and operating-company descriptors. It distinguishes replacement of a damaged side when other decals meet current schematics from replacement of all sides when the remaining decals do not meet current schematics. It permits good-condition Ground or purple/green decals while requiring inconsistent logos across sides to be replaced to match current schematics. It assigns new-decal supply after an accident/incident to FedEx and reapplication cost to the service provider.

Page 2 assigns removal cost for damaged, faded, peeling, or cracked decals to the service provider; describes clean-vehicle appearance in terms of customer expectations; identifies the current Service Provider Profile business name as the display basis subject to FedEx trademark/brand determinations; permits `Inc.` and `Corp.` abbreviations; and identifies Lowen or another brand-compliant vendor for business-name decals.

The complete source establishes contractor branding and vehicle-administration responsibilities, not a general driver-at-stop procedure. Archive: `sources/mygroundbiz/documents/Vehicle-Appearance-and-Brand-Promotion-FAQ172026.pdf`.
