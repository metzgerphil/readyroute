# SRC-MGB-PAGE-0033 — Safety Results Summary / Safety Results Indicator

- Review date: 2026-08-09

- Coverage: complete visible authenticated page
- Status: fully reviewed; contextual only
- Durable capture: `captures/mygroundbiz/srs_sri.txt`

The page defines SRS as a 36-month summary of a service provider's accidents, roadside inspections, disconnects, improper assemblies, pull-offs, and VEDR key indicators. It defines SRI as a number and tier generated from the most recent rolling 12 months to indicate business safety performance.

Those definitions may matter to contractor management and analytics, but the page does not establish what an individual driver should do in response to a score or event. No driver procedure is promoted from the page. The linked English and French FAQs are separately inventoried and remain unreviewed.
