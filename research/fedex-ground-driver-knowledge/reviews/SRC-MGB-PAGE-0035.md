# SRC-MGB-PAGE-0035 — Trailer Pull Safety at FedEx, Customer Locations

- Review date: 2026-08-09
- Published: 2022-02-11
- Portal update: 2022-08-02
- Coverage: complete visible authenticated page
- Status: fully reviewed
- Durable capture: `captures/mygroundbiz/trailer_pull_safety.txt`

The live page requires operators to examine cargo and load-securement devices, ensure cargo is properly loaded and secure, and get out for a visual inspection before operating. Immediately before pulling a trailer, personnel must visually confirm dock plates are down or detached and that trailer and dock doors are closed. If direct visual confirmation is unavailable, the page permits a back-door tap or consideration of a digital camera probe. At customer locations, the operator should attempt customer confirmation; if the trailer still cannot be verified, notify FedEx. If neither customer nor FedEx can verify readiness, leave the trailer and do not pull it.

The linked English/French flyers and candidate versions are separately inventoried and remain unreviewed. Three historical safety links resolve to explicit Sitecore not-found endpoints and are inventoried as inaccessible. No unseen flyer or alert content is inferred.
