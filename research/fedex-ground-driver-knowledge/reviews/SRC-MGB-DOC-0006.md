# SRC-MGB-DOC-0006 — OP-130 Accident Packet Instructions

- Review date: 2026-08-08
- Document date: 8/2025
- Coverage: 1/1 page, visual review
- Status: fully reviewed

The packet must be kept in the vehicle glove compartment with any rental agreement. It directs the driver to call 9-1-1 if needed; move off the traveled roadway/shoulder when the vehicle is on the traveled portion or shoulder, using required FMCSR 392.22 warning devices unless exempt; notify local police; contact the service provider authorized officer/business contact and notify FedEx as soon as possible under the circumstances; record license plate numbers; protect the vehicle/cargo until protective measures can be taken; wait at the scene for the adjuster if required; and complete the accident packet and any city/state reports FedEx recommends.

Station and service-provider emergency numbers are blank fields and must be locally completed. The current Accident Reporting page additionally identifies the information representatives should provide and links OP-132 and OP-135.
