## Approved scope

1. Add the 49 CFR 393.75 tire-tread thresholds to the unified vehicle-inspection answer: at least 4/32 inch on front/steering tires and at least 2/32 inch on other tires, measured in a major tread groove. The penny test may be taught only as a rough 2/32-inch screen and must not be presented as proof that a steer tire meets 4/32 inch.
2. Upgrade same-day misdelivery recovery with the supplied scan-level Code 17, address verification, normal delivery, and successful Code 18 mechanics.
3. Define WA as Work Area, another word for the route, found as the first three or four numbers on the Vision Label/SID sticker.
4. Add the supplied signature-capture mechanics: after the delivery check mark, the signature box appears when a signature is required or Met Customer is selected; collect the signature and enter first initial plus full last name.
5. For a customer asking to pay cash on delivery, have the customer call the driver's BC. This is not approval of a more detailed COD procedure.
6. Distinguish the conceptual DSR-versus-ISR explanation from the operational signature-service selector. DSR requires an in-person signature at the labeled address and cannot use a neighbor at another address; ISR can allow an eligible neighbor signature with the required indirect-delivery and door-tag documentation.
7. When a torn label's barcode still scans and the package itself is not damaged, deliver normally. If the package is damaged, use the damaged-package procedure.
8. Treat an informal lunch-break or lunch-logging question as company-specific and direct the driver to ask the BC. Keep that answer separate from federal HOS break-limit questions.
9. Recognize “hazmat placard on the package” as driver wording for a diamond-shaped hazardous-material package label or marking. Explain that it identifies a hazardous-material hazard/class and requires the applicable hazmat paperwork and procedure rather than ordinary-package handling.
10. Document canceling an accidental delivered scan before it finalizes as a separate mechanic from Delete Scan for a wrong package already committed to Stop Details.
11. Treat “Vision Label” and “SID sticker” as equivalent terminology throughout active driver-facing knowledge.

## Scope limits and reopen conditions

- Do not invent a more complete COD procedure or company lunch policy.
- Do not merge pre-finalization cancel mechanics with the committed-scan Delete Scan procedure or with completed misdelivery recovery.
- Package-specific signature, hazmat, damage, company-policy, legal, and safety requirements remain controlling.
- Reopen any determination if Phillip or Vlad revises it or later applicable operational, regulatory, company-policy, or safety guidance materially conflicts with it.
