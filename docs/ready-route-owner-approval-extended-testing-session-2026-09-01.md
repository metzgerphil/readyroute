# Ready Route owner approval — Extended testing operational additions

**Field source:** Vlad

## Approval

After receiving the ReadyRoute Extended Testing Session Report and being asked to approve Vlad’s supplied customer-complaint, scanner-low-battery, and pickup-window procedures as owner-verified Ready Route knowledge, Phillip Metzger responded: “I approve.”

## Approved scope

1. For a general customer complaint, give the customer the driver’s BC phone contact, then have the driver call the BC and explain what is happening.
2. For a scanner that is dying or low on battery: swap in an extra battery if available; FORGE will not log the driver out during that battery swap. If no extra battery is available, call the BC. If the driver is far from another driver and no nearby spare battery is available, use a hand sheet (blue sheet) to record deliveries and turn it in at the check-in office.
3. When the driver may miss a pickup window, retain the existing steps to check the current ready and close times and contact CXPC so the customer can be alerted. Also contact the BC to see whether the pickup can be transferred to another Work Area.

## Scope limits

- A general customer complaint must not be treated as a CXPC Pickup Research Request unless the facts establish that CXPC sent a request about a reported missed pickup.
- The scanner-low-battery procedure applies while the device is losing power. It does not silently replace the separate procedures for a frozen scanner, an app crash, a fully unavailable scanner, or a pickup scanning-technology failure.
- The pickup-window addition authorizes asking the BC whether a transfer is possible. It does not state that a transfer is guaranteed, and it does not remove the CXPC notification step.
- No procedure is approved here for duplicate packages or two packages sharing a tracking number. That item remains pending field confirmation.
- The report’s routing and fallback observations are engineering evidence, not independent operational authority.

## Reopen conditions

- Phillip or Vlad revises a supplied procedure.
- A later applicable operational, company-policy, scanner-workflow, safety, or regulatory update materially conflicts with an approved determination.
- A more specific complaint, device-failure, pickup-transfer, or package-identity scenario establishes a different approved procedure.
