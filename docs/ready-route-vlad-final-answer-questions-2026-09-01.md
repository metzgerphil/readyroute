**Driver question:** “Smoke is coming from the van. What do I do?”

Please confirm the immediate sequence: stopping, exiting, emergency contact, vehicle or package handling restrictions, BC/station notification, and required reporting. Identify whether visible fire, engine smoke, cargo smoke, or an unknown source changes the response.

**Driver question:** “I feel sick and dizzy while driving. What do I do?”

Please confirm the immediate driving and parking actions, when 911 or medical help is required, who the driver contacts, and what happens to the vehicle and route.

**Driver question:** “Power lines are down near the delivery.”

**Driver question:** “I have a flat tire on route. What do I do?”
