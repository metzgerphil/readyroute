# Ready Route owner-verified knowledge policy

## Decision

Effective 2026-08-15, Ready Route Answers recognizes two valid paths to operational publication:

1. **Document-verified** — supported by reviewed documentary evidence and published as `SOURCE_VERIFIED`.
2. **Owner-verified** — explicitly supplied or confirmed as valid by the Ready Route product owner and published as `READY_ROUTE_APPROVED` through an adjudication.

A PDF, manual, or other external document is not required for owner-verified knowledge. The lack of a matching document must not by itself block a useful answer that the product owner has verified.

## Controls that remain

- Preserve the exact information or procedure the owner supplied.
- Record the approver, approval date, provenance, reasoning, and reopen conditions.
- Do not add plausible steps, extend the procedure to a new situation, or use general model knowledge to fill omissions.
- Preserve conflicts and older interpretations rather than deleting them.
- If later information materially challenges an owner-approved procedure, reopen it for review rather than silently replacing it.
- Explicitly pending or uncertain information remains pending unless the owner separately verifies it.

## Initial application

The product owner's 2026-08-15 direction approves the operational information supplied through the partner session report, including its field-verified procedures, even where the report lacks a documentary citation. The report's explicitly pending Code 20 item remains pending because the supplied information itself says not to add it until confirmed.
