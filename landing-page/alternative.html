Common questions

What contractors should know.

Does ReadyRoute replace required training?

No. ReadyRoute is an operational-reference system for trained personnel. It does not replace required training or create authority for unsupported procedures.

What happens when an answer is not supported?

ReadyRoute does not guess. It identifies that verified guidance is unavailable and directs the driver to the appropriate manager or station escalation.
