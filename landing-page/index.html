Company setup

A guided rollout from first conversation to active drivers.

ReadyRoute is configured as a company workspace, not handed to drivers as an unstructured public tool. The rollout establishes ownership, approved information, access, and a measurable pilot.

1

Request a company workspace

Tell us who you are, your company or CSA information, estimated driver count, and the operational problems you want ReadyRoute to help solve.

Outcome: setup request created
2

Confirm the rollout owner and boundaries

We identify the primary company administrator, staff roles, applicable operating areas, and which authorized reference materials can support your team.

Outcome: accountable company owner
3

Configure staff and driver access

Your workspace is created, managers and dispatchers receive portal access, and drivers are invited into the correct company environment.

Outcome: controlled access
4

Run a focused pilot

A smaller driver group tests real questions, image references, feedback controls, and escalation behavior before a wider launch.

Outcome: verified launch readiness
5

Expand and review performance

Add the rest of the team, monitor adoption and answer quality, and use failed or unhelpful questions to guide the next knowledge improvements.

Outcome: measurable ongoing program

Common questions

What contractors should know before launch.

Does ReadyRoute replace required training?

No. ReadyRoute is an operational-reference system for trained personnel. It does not replace required training or create authority for unsupported procedures.

What happens when an answer is not supported?

ReadyRoute does not guess. It identifies that verified guidance is unavailable and directs the driver to the appropriate manager or station escalation.

Can contractors see whether it is working?

Yes. Staff reporting includes question activity, verified outcomes, failures, driver feedback, and trends by company and user.

How long does company setup take?

The timeline depends on company structure, user readiness, and the reference material that must be reviewed. The process starts with a short readiness conversation.
