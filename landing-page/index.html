Why ReadyRoute

Built for the way contractors actually run the day.

ReadyRoute helps contractors load routes, assign drivers, push work to the app, and monitor the fleet from one clean manager portal.

Save money

Flat pricing without setup fees or annual contracts.

Save time

Load routes, assign drivers, and get the day moving faster.

Stay in control

Track route progress, GPS freshness, exceptions, and driver activity.

How to get started
1

Set up your account

Create your ReadyRoute workspace, add your CSA, and invite managers or dispatchers.

2

Connect FCC

Connect your approved FedEx Customer Connection data feed so route data can flow into ReadyRoute.

3

Assign and dispatch

Match routes to drivers and vehicles, then send the day's work to the driver app.

4

Monitor live

Track route progress, completed stops, GPS freshness, and exceptions from the manager dashboard.

Features

The tools your dispatch day needs.

Manifest-first route control

Keep route execution centered on the day's manifest.

Driver app with stop details

Give drivers route order, stop details, notes, and package information.

Live fleet dashboard

VEDR access

Driver and vehicle management

Manage drivers, vehicles, assignments, and route readiness.
