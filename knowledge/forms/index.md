# Forms and documentation

The maintained artifact ledger is [`form_artifact_coverage.csv`](../../research/fedex-ground-driver-knowledge/knowledge/form_artifact_coverage.csv), with its audit at [`form_and_physical_artifact_coverage_audit.md`](../../research/fedex-ground-driver-knowledge/validation/form_and_physical_artifact_coverage_audit.md).

It covers discovered driver-used forms, door tags, signature/release records, call tags, hand sheets, labels, manifests, credentials, and related physical documentation. Artifact identity, current revision, access, procedure completeness, and publication eligibility are separate decisions. Colloquial wording such as “Blue Sheet” may retrieve candidates but cannot establish the official form or unlock unsupported instructions.
